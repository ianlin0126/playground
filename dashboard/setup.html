Telegram Bot

Your kid will chat with the guardian through a Telegram bot you create. It only takes 2 minutes.

  1. Open Telegram and search for @BotFather
  2. Send /newbot and follow the prompts
  3. Copy the token BotFather gives you (looks like 1234567890:AAH...)

🔒 Your bot token is saved only to the local .env file on this machine and is never transmitted anywhere else. You can also add it manually: open .env in the playground directory and add KID_BOT_TOKEN=your-token-here
